How it works

Three steps, zero paperwork

No account, no OTP, no document upload. Just tell us a little about yourself and get a straight answer.

01

Answer five quick questions

Age, income, occupation, gender and state — the same details every scheme actually checks for.

02

Our rule engine checks instantly

Plain if/else logic — not AI, not guesswork — compares your answers against each scheme's published criteria.

03

See your matches, save what matters

Get a ranked list with plain-language explainers, and bookmark the ones you want to act on later.
